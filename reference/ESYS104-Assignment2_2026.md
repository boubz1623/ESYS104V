**Figure description:** Pangea map with continents labelled North America, Africa, South America, Eurasia, India, Antarctica, Australia. Letter positions:

- **A**: open ocean (Panthalassa) on the equator, far west of the supercontinent.
- **B**: ocean at about 45–50° N, off the northwest coast of North America.
- **C**: in the Tethys Ocean on the equator, east of Africa and south of Eurasia.
- **D**: ocean just south of 30° N (about 20–25° N), just off the west coast of North America.
- **E**: on land on the equator, at the eastern edge of Africa beside the Tethys.
- **F**: on land at about 10° N, on the western side of North America, south of B and D. The Appalachian mountain symbols (^) run from about 30° N down toward the equator, east of F, between North America and Africa.

c) In terms of temperature and precipitation, what is the climate at location 'E'?

d) What is the climate at location 'F'
