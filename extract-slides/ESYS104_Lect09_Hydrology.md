### Extracted text

```text
The water balance equation
Applied to an entire drainage basin over entire year:
P=Et+R     
(i.e. water inputs = outputs, averaged over 12 months)  
(units of volume of water per year)
• P = annual precipitation.
• Et = annual actual evapotranspiration.
• R = Runoff (flow of liquid river water from land via streams, 
available to use; includes groundwater).
Or: 
 R = P- Et  
i.e. even if it rains, available water R is decreased if Et is very 
high (in hot, dry climates)
```

### Visual transcription / extraction note

Diagram: **How is soil made? – weathering**. Parent material is broken down by mechanical weathering (wind, rain, hail, ice, frost, heat, cold), spontaneous weathering, and chemical weathering (acids, moisture) into fine parent material.
