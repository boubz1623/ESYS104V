```text
Atmosphere Energy Balance
```

### Visual transcription / extraction note

Equations (image text):

- Incoming solar shortwave radiation: S₀/4
- Solar energy reflected back into space: the albedo α
- Outgoing surface energy: σTₑ⁴
- Surface longwave energy absorbed by the atmosphere: εₐσTₑ⁴
- Rest of the surface longwave energy not absorbed by the atmosphere: (1−εₐ)σTₑ⁴
- Atmospheric longwave energy emitted: εₐσTₐ⁴
- Earth's surface energy balance: **S₀/4 (1−α) + εₐσTₐ⁴ = σTₑ⁴**

![Slide 51](<assets/ESYS104_Lect06_Atmos_Circ/slide-051.png>)

### Extracted text

```text
Jet Streams
• The temperature contrast between the poles and 
equator drives the westerly winds (westerlies) located 
in the middle latitudes. 
• Imbedded within the westerly flow aloft are narrow 
ribbons of high-speed winds, called jet streams, that 
meander for thousands of kilometers. 
• The key to the origin of midlatitude jet streams is found 
in great temperature contrasts at the surface. 
• In the midlatitudes between 30 and 70°, a polar jet 
stream occurs in association with the polar front.
```
