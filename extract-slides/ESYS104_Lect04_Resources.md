```text
How long is CO2 stored?--The Residence Time
The average duration an element or molecule is stored in a 
reservoir at steady state conditions is named the “residence 
time”. This average time is calculated by dividing the mass of 
the element or molecule by either the flux into, or out of, the 
reservoir (they’re the same at steady state). Or using math:
Residence time =  __m__
           dm/dt
In continental rocks the residence time of carbon is ~ 150 Ma.
  
On the other hand carbon in soils (contain both calcite and 
organic carbon) has a residence time of about 25 years.
```
