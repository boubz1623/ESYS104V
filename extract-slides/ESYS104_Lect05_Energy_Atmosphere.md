[Original source](<../slides/ESYS104_Lect05_Energy_Atmosphere.pdf#page=45>)

![Slide 45](<assets/ESYS104_Lect05_Energy_Atmosphere/slide-045.png>)

### Extracted text

```text
Atmosphere Energy Balance
```

### Visual transcription / extraction note

Full transcription of the image-based formula list (subscripts follow this slide):

- Incoming solar shortwave radiation: $S_0/4$.
- Solar energy reflected back into space: the albedo $\alpha$.
- Outgoing surface energy: $\sigma T_e^4$.
- Surface longwave energy absorbed by the atmosphere: $\epsilon_a\sigma T_e^4$.
- Rest of the surface longwave energy not absorbed by the atmosphere: $(1-\epsilon_a)\sigma T_e^4$.
- Atmospheric longwave energy emitted: $\epsilon_a\sigma T_a^4$.

Earth’s surface energy balance:

$$\frac{S_0}{4}(1-\alpha)+\epsilon_a\sigma T_a^4=\sigma T_e^4.$$
