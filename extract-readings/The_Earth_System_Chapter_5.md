Upwelling and Downwelling
In areas Of convergence, the surface water piles up, the sea
surface rises, and the surface layer of water thickens (Figure

upwelling. As we will see later in this chapter, these deeper
waters also tend to be rich in nutrients. Upwelling, therefore,
brings these nutrient-rich waters to the surface.
