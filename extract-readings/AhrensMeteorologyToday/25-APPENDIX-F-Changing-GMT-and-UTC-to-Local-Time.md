The system of time used in meteorology is Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), which is also known as Coor-
dinated Universal Time (UTC), and as Zulu (Z) Time. This is the time measured on the prime meridian 
(0° longitude) in Greenwich, England. Because Eastern Standard Time (EST) is 5 hours slower than GMT, 
to convert from GMT to EST simply requires subtracting 5 hours from GMT. Conversely, to change EST 
to GMT entails adding 5 hours to EST. Figure F.1 shows how to convert to GMT in various time zones of 
North America. Since in meteorology the time is given on a 24-clock, Table F.1 shows the relationship 
between the familiar two 12-hour periods of a.m. and p.m. and the 24-hour system.
